Synthetic document. Customer orders follow four stages: quote confirmation, commercial approval, provisioning, and activation handover. Standard provisioning completes within five business days; expedited provisioning within two business days requires operations approval. Order changes after commercial approval need a new quote reference, and cancelled orders are archived with the cancellation reason for quarterly review. Invoicing starts only after the activation handover is confirmed by the customer contact.
