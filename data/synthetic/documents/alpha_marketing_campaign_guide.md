Synthetic document. The marketing team plans campaigns per quarter across the channels email, social, search, events, and partner referral. Every campaign requires a documented target segment, a UTM tracking plan, and a double-opt-in consent basis before any outreach. Lead scoring combines firmographic fit, engagement signals, and product interest; leads above the qualification threshold are handed to sales with the campaign source attached. Campaign reporting shows cost per qualified lead and conversion per channel and must never include personal contact data.
